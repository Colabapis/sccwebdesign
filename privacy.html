Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026.

SCC Web Design respects your privacy and only collects information needed to respond to enquiries, provide services and improve this website.

Information we collect

If you contact us, we may collect your name, email address and project details. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics may collect information about how visitors use the website, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To provide and manage agreed web design or development services.
  • To understand website performance and improve the visitor experience.
  • To meet legal, tax and business record obligations.

Lawful basis

We process enquiry and client information where it is necessary for legitimate interests, contract discussions or service delivery. Analytics cookies are used only with your consent.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers where required for hosting, email, analytics, accounting or legal compliance.

Data retention

Enquiry information is kept only as long as needed for communication and reasonable business records. Client records may be retained for legal and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of personal information, and you can withdraw analytics cookie consent by clearing your browser storage for this site. To make a privacy request, email hello@sccwebdesign.co.uk.
