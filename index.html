SCC method

Search.
Corroborate.
Communicate.

A design studio still benefits from research discipline. Understand the business and its audience, verify the important technical assumptions, then communicate with clarity.

Validation

Does it work?

Routes, forms, mobile layouts, accessibility, metadata and deployment checks are part of delivery rather than an afterthought.

Verification

Is it really what we think it is?

Migration and modernisation projects compare source and replacement instead of trusting a superficial visual impression.

Integrity

Say what is live — and what is not.

Work in progress stays labelled as work in progress. Case studies do not invent metrics, launches or client outcomes.

Provenance

Know where the work came from.

Source assets, build history and deployment routes remain auditable, helping future maintenance stay controlled.
